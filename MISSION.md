# Mission: Salary Deductions, EPFO & TDS Optimization for Indian Employees

## Why
Structure salary deductions, eliminate tax withholding surprises (TDS), recover excess deducted tax via ITR, and properly allocate cash flows between sovereign fixed income and equity funds.

## Success looks like
- Calculate exact EPF, EPS, and monthly TDS withholdings from any CTC breakdown.
- Predict and regulate monthly TDS cuts by properly declaring Form 12BB investments.
- Reconcile Form 16, Form 26AS, and AIS to claim refunds on excess deducted TDS.
- Determine whether to limit EPF to statutory ceiling (₹15,000 / ₹25,000) or contribute on full basic salary.
- Protect accumulated corpus from TDS and income tax during job switches (the 5-year continuous service rule).
- Prevent taxable interest leakage under Section 10(11)/(12) (>₹2.5L annual employee contribution cap).

## Constraints
- Communication must be extremely concise and direct.
- Explain concepts using intuitive layman analogies before mechanics.

## Out of scope
- Employer-side statutory filings and establishment audits.
- International worker bilateral social security treaties.
- Exempted PF trust accounting under Section 17.
