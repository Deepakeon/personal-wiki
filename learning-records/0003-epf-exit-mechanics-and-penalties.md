# EPF Exit Options, Penalties & The 5-Year Rule

Established exit taxonomy: Form 19 (full EPF), Form 10C (EPS cash/certificate), Form 31 (advances), Form 10D (pension). Clarified the primary penalty: premature withdrawal before 5 years continuous service triggers Section 192A TDS (10%/20%), reverses Section 80C deductions, and taxes entire employer share + interest as salary. Transferred service via UAN aggregates toward the 5-year threshold.
