# Corrected Misconception: FD Nominal Yield vs EPF Post-Tax Compounding

User believed a 7.15% quarterly-compounded FD (8.38x nominal over 30 yrs) outperformed EPF's 8.15%–8.25%. Corrected by factoring in annual tax drag: FD interest is taxed at slab (effective 4.92% post-tax @ 30% slab = 4.34x), whereas EPF is EEE tax-free (10.79x). Established that EPF delivers >2.4x the post-tax return of an FD, even before counting employer matching contributions.
