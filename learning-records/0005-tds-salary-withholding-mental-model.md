# TDS (Tax Deducted at Source) Mental Model for Employees

User extended learning scope from EPF to TDS withholding mechanics. Established foundational mental model: TDS is not an extra tax, but an advance installment toward annual tax liability under Section 192. Unlocks lessons on Form 12BB declarations, Form 16 reconciliation, 26AS/AIS verification, and refund retrieval via ITR filing.
