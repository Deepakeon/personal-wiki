# Uncovered Employers, Startups & Entrepreneurship Transitions

Established rules when transitioning to an establishment with <20 employees or founding a startup:
1. Account continues earning interest until age 58 (GSR 1065(E)), but post-resignation interest is taxable at slab.
2. Full withdrawal (Form 19 + 10C) is permitted after 60 days of non-contributory status.
3. If prior service >= 5 years, withdrawal is 100% tax-free. If < 5 years, full withdrawal triggers Section 192A tax penalty.
4. Establishments with < 20 employees can opt into voluntary coverage under Section 1(4).
