TDS Quick Cheat Sheet for Employees

Essential layman reference for understanding Tax Deducted at Source.

1. Key Income Tax Sections Deducting Money From You

Section Income Type Rate Threshold / Rule
Sec 192 Monthly Salary Slab rate Calculated on estimated annual net income divided by 12.
Sec 192A EPF Withdrawal 10% (20% no PAN) Triggered if withdrawing balance before 5 years service & amount > ₹50,000.
Sec 194A Bank FD Interest 10% (20% no PAN) Triggered if bank interest exceeds ₹40,000/yr (₹50,000 for senior citizens).
Sec 194J Freelance / Professional 10% (or 2%) If contract/consulting fees exceed ₹30,000/yr from a single payer.

2. Essential Forms Every Salaried Employee Must Know

  • Form 12BB: Investment declaration form you submit to HR (rent receipts, 80C/80D proofs) by Dec/Jan to prevent heavy TDS cuts in Q4.
  • Form 16: Annual tax certificate issued by your employer by June 15. Proves exact salary paid and TDS deposited with the government.
  • Form 26AS & AIS (Annual Information Statement): Central government tax passbook on the e-filing portal. Shows every rupee of TDS deposited against your PAN.
  • ITR (Income Tax Return): Annual filing to reconcile total taxes vs total TDS. Generates refund if TDS deducted was more than actual liability.
