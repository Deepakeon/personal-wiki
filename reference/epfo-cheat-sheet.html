EPFO Quick Cheat Sheet

Essential layman reference for Indian salaried employees.

1. Monthly Money Split (Total 24% of Basic)

Source Share Where It Goes What It Does
You (Employee) 12% EPF (Your lockbox) 100% yours. Grows at ~8.15% compound interest.
Employer 3.67% EPF (Your lockbox) Added to your private lockbox with interest.
Employer 8.33% EPS (Pension pool) Capped at ₹1,250/mo. Not your capital; pooled for ₹1k–₹3.5k monthly pension post age 58.
Employer 0.50% EDLI (Life insurance) Free life insurance up to ₹7 Lakh for family on death during service.

2. Golden Rules to Protect Money

  • Never withdraw on job switch: Always transfer via UAN. Withdrawing before 5 years total service triggers heavy retroactive tax and 10% TDS.
  • ₹2.5 Lakh Annual Limit: If your own employee contribution exceeds ₹2.5 Lakh/year, interest on excess is taxed at income slab.
  • Excluded Employee Advantage: If your basic pay at your first job was >₹15,000, you can ask HR to put full 12% employer share into EPF lockbox, skipping the dead-end EPS pool.
  • Claims: Form 19 (full EPF), Form 10C (EPS cash/certificate), Form 31 (emergency non-refundable advance).
