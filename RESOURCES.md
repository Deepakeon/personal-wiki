# EPFO Resources

## Knowledge

- [EPFO Official Portal](https://www.epfindia.gov.in)
  Official statutory authority portal. Use for: circulars, annual interest rate declarations, service rules.
- [EPFO Member Unified Portal](https://unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in/memberinterface/)
  Self-service portal for employees. Use for: passbook download, KYC seeding, online claims (Form 19/10C/31), auto-transfer tracking.
- [Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952](https://www.epfindia.gov.in/site_docs/PDFs/Downloads_PDFs/EPFAct1952.pdf)
  Primary statute governing EPF, EPS, EDLI. Use for: legal mandates, court protection (Sec 10), eligibility ceilings.
- [Income Tax Department - Rules on PF (Rule 9D & Section 192A)](https://incometaxindia.gov.in)
  Statutory tax code. Use for: 5-year continuous service rule, ₹2.5L interest taxation threshold, TDS deductions.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [r/IndiaInvestments](https://reddit.com/r/IndiaInvestments)
  High-signal Indian personal finance community. Use for: real-world claim rejection troubleshooting, asset allocation discussions, EPF vs PPF/NPS debates.
- [EPFiGMS (EPFO Grievance Management System)](https://epfigms.gov.in)
  Official escalation portal for delayed claims, employer non-remittance, and KYC rejection resolution.

- [Income Tax e-Filing Portal](https://eportal.incometax.gov.in)
  Official tax filing and AIS/Form 26AS portal. Use for: checking deposited TDS credits, filing ITR, claiming refunds.
- [TRACES Portal (TDS Reconciliation Analysis and Correction Enabling System)](https://contents.tdscpc.gov.in)
  Directorate of Income Tax portal for TDS certificates. Use for: Form 16 Part A verification.
- [Section 192 of Income Tax Act, 1961](https://incometaxindia.gov.in)
  Governing statute for salary tax withholding by employers.
